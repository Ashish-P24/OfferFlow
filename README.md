# 🚀 OfferFlow

A production-oriented full-stack career management platform that helps users organize and track their job applications, interviews, offers, and career progress.

---

## ✨ Features

- Secure User Authentication (JWT)
- Job Application Tracking
- Interview Management
- Resume & Cover Letter Management
- Dashboard & Analytics
- Search & Filtering
- Email Reminders
- Notes & Attachments

---
